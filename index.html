1 About/

GOPHER is a paid SaaS phone VA that sits in front of your Grok Bot. You talk to a number. GOPHER turns the talk into an order, posts it over a webhook, and brings the answer back to the same thread.

The name is the point. Old Gopher was a menu, a selector, a fetch. No infinite scroll. No chat sludge. GOPHER keeps that contract.

GOPHER is not an official Grok Bot product. There is no official Grok Bot public API. We talk to the engine you already run, via webhooks and plugins you control.

1 How it works/

  1. You send an order from the phone — voice or SMS.
  2. GOPHER posts that order to a webhook your Grok Bot instance already understands.
  3. Grok Bot does the work through plugins you’ve wired: lookup, calendar, draft, notify.
  4. GOPHER delivers the result back to the same conversation.

1 Capabilities/

  • 0 Voice and SMS in. A reply out.
  • 0 Orders, not chat: fetch this, remind me, what’s on Tuesday.
  • 0 Webhook + plugin routing to the Grok Bot you run.
  • 0 A log of what was asked and what came back.

Not a general chatbot. Not a free public menu. Not a way to scrape your phone.

0 Privacy

We keep your waitlist email now; later, your number and order logs to do the job. We don’t sell them.
