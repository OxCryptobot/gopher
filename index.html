1 About/

GOPHER AI is a standalone phone assistant you pay for. You talk to a number. It turns the talk into an order, fetches, and brings the answer back to the same thread.

The name is the point. Old Gopher was a menu, a selector, a fetch. No infinite scroll. No chat sludge. GOPHER AI keeps that contract and puts a modern prompt on top of it.

2 How it works/

  1. You send an order from the phone — voice, SMS, or this prompt.
  2. GOPHER AI files it as a selector: fetch this, watch that, draft, remind.
  3. Plugins you connect do the work: calendar, mail, files, markets, the rest.
  4. The result comes back to the same conversation. You never open twelve tabs.

3 Capabilities/

  • 0 Voice and SMS in. A reply out.
  • 0 Orders, not chat: fetch, watch, remind, draft.
  • 0 Numbered menus + a modern ask box. Keyboard-first.
  • 0 A log of what was asked and what came back.

Not a general chatbot. Not a free public menu. Not a way to scrape your phone.

4 Privacy

We keep your waitlist email now; later, your number and order logs to do the job. We don’t sell them.
